# Panam

### Your personal finance, all in one place.

Panam is a personal finance management app designed to give users a complete view of their financial life — from everyday spending and credit card bills to investments, insurance, recurring payments, and debt.

## ✦ Overview

Managing personal finances often means jumping between multiple apps, bank statements, investment platforms, and reminders.

**Panam brings it all together.**

Track where your money comes from, where it goes, what you owe, what you're owed, what you own, and what's coming next — all from one place.

## ✦ Features

* 💰 **Cash Flow** — Track income, expenses, and spending by category.
* 🏦 **Accounts** — Manage bank accounts, cash, wallets, and balances.
* 💳 **Credit Cards** — Track transactions, outstanding bills, due dates, and debt.
* 🔁 **Recurring Payments** — Keep track of subscriptions, AutoPays, EMIs, and recurring expenses.
* 📈 **Investments** — Track monthly/quarterly investments and overall portfolio value.
* 🛡️ **Insurance** — Manage policies, premiums, renewal dates, and upcoming payments.
* 🤝 **Lending & Debt** — Track money you owe and money others owe you.
* 📊 **Financial Overview** — Understand income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and net worth at a glance.

## ✦ The Goal

Panam isn't just an expense tracker.

It's designed to become a **single source of truth for your personal finances** — helping you understand your current financial position and stay ahead of upcoming commitments.

## ✦ Design

Built with a focus on:

* Simple financial visualization
* Clear information hierarchy
* Minimal cognitive load
* Fast everyday interactions
* A consolidated financial dashboard
